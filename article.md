### 3 ways to apply generative AI to Talent Management
#### Enterprises that are employing generative artificial intelligence to recruit, train, and evaluate employees.
Employees are the building blocks of any successful business. Productivity can exhibit a compounding effect at any workplace irrespective of business nature.

A higher degree of productivity at a lower level can result in exceeding output on a higher level. It is of utmost importance to measure the productivity of employees for a sustainable business. Performance measurement and quantification of employees can help in the accurate assessment of developmental patterns. Employees cannot be assessed solely on a single quality as there are many attributes to consider. Skillset and behavioral characteristics are among the variable factors. The machine learning algorithm can be applied to assess the wide attributes of any employee.

Developing a data mining framework is the first step towards the evaluation process. Many industries-tested techniques are available for data mining. These classification techniques include but are not limited to the fuzzy set approach, genetic algorithms, decision trees, support vector machines, rough set theory, and neural networks. The decision tree data mining framework is widely accepted due to its accurate output. A decision tree works by generating a tree and a set of rules about any given dataset. Enterprises that are employing artificial intelligence are leveraging their machine and cognitive potentials. Generative AI is an assurance of solid organizational knowledge and optimum data processing which mimics the human capabilities of going about any problem. As the industry is shifting the paradigm to AI; several enterprises around the globe are following the trend by structuring the data in a machine-readable manner.

#### Employee Performance Evaluation by Generative AI
The core objective behind the performance evaluation is to assess the performance trends and what are the capabilities of the employee in the future. This is not solely a measure of individual performance but also the potential of any employee within a team to achieve organizational objectives. A well-structured and assorted data is an essential part of the PE. Data-driven outcomes are the assurance of organization stability and innovation. In generative AI-powered PE methods, data is acquired categorically and automatically by the algorithm from several sources. Continuous collection of the data can also help in a dynamic PE for any employee or a team. Dynamic PE can be used to perform the minor course adjustments more frequently. Automated data collection also eliminates the possibility of human error and provides a presentable review. Despite such extensive inputs, the dynamic PE is by far time-saving when compared to periodic performance evaluation of the employees.

#### Employee Development by Generative AI
In the fast-paced era of today, a number of technological innovations are culprits for obsoleting human skill sets. This in turn is widening the skill gap for the employees. AI-Enabled solutions employed in the workplace are an assurance of optimum learning and development (L&D) for employees. The positive points worth mentioning are automation, intuitive and custom-tailored content which catalyzes the continuous L&D. Dynamic learning and improvement serves the purpose of achieving the maximum potential of any employee within the enterprise. Generative AI can predict the learning behaviors of any employee and presenting suitable methods and content to maximize the learning outcome.

Chatbots are commonly known counterparts that are employed to train employees for different activities. The generative AI-powered learning models also eliminate the bias in learning and leniency of any manager to a certain employee. Organizations can achieve ROI in minimal time with well-experienced personnel. Integration of AI with the LMSs is by far the sustainable model there is available in the market for learning and development. Employees can learn the skills that are required in the current time as well as the time to come to stay in the market. Learning experience platform (LXP) is the latest example of one such platform available in the market.

#### Generative AI to Hire New Employees
Long gone are the days when recruiters used to go through piles of resumes a day searching for the right candidate. One of the major problems encountered in HRM is the evaluation of resumes. As per a report, about 75% of resumes received for any given role are of no relevance. AI has revolutionized the way HRM hires new people while drastically cutting down on the timeframe of hiring a new employee possessing a definitive set of skills. Generative AI-powered programs are capable of going through extensive data to finalize the candidate as per the required set of skills and other personal attributes.

With generative AI-enabled platforms, recruiters can automate most of the tedious tasks involved in the hiring of employees such as job posting, potential applicants, work history of potential employees, and other such data. The generative AI is also being employed by the talent hunt sites such as LinkedIn to present the matching candidates with the Job requirement. Such programs make use of candidates' personal information and professional details to generate the results using ML. Similar algorithms are also available at the recruiter's disposal to find the optimum candidates from the total applicants in any job listing.
