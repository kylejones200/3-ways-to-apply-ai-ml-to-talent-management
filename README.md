## Business context

Employees are the building blocks of any successful business. Productivity can exhibit a compounding effect at any workplace irrespective of business nature.

A higher degree of productivity at a lower level can result in exceeding output on a higher level. It is of utmost importance to measure the productivity of employees for a sustainable business. Performance measurement and quantification of employees can help in the accurate assessment of developmental patterns. Employees cannot be assessed solely on a single quality as there are many attributes to consider. Skillset and behavioral characteristics are among the variable factors. The machine learning algorithm can be applied to assess the wide attributes of any employee.

Developing a data mining framework is the first step towards the evaluation process. Many industries-tested techniques are available for data mining. These classification techniques include but are not limited to the fuzzy set approach, genetic algorithms, decision trees, support vector machines, rough set theory, and neural networks. The decision tree data mining framework is widely accepted due to its accurate output. A decision tree works by generating a tree and a set of rules about any given dataset.
